🌍 How to Build Your World

  • Select a building from the toolbar below, then click any empty tile on the grid to place it.
  • Erase a tile by selecting the 🗑️ Erase tool, then clicking the tile you want to remove.
  • Expand your world by earning Fish 🐟 in mini-games and clicking the Expand button.
  • New buildings unlock by completing Story chapters or buying from the Shop.
  • Your progress is auto-saved — it will be here when you come back!

📖 How Story Mode Works

  • Each chapter gives you tasks to complete on your island — like placing certain buildings or reaching a population.
  • When all tasks are done, click Claim Reward to get Fish, Stars, and unlock new buildings!
  • Completing a chapter unlocks the next one — follow Pebble the penguin's journey to build a legendary isle.
📖 Chronicles of Penguin Isle

🎮 How Mini-games Work

  • Play any mini-game to earn Fish 🐟 — the main currency for buying buildings and expanding your world.
  • Each game has its own controls explained when you open it. You can replay them as many times as you want!
  • The Penguin Identifier quiz tests your real penguin knowledge — get it right for bonus fish!

🛒 How the Shop Works

  • Spend Fish 🐟 (earned from mini-games) to permanently unlock buildings for your toolbar.
  • Once bought, you can place that building on your island as many times as you like — for free!
  • Some buildings are only unlockable through Story chapters, not the shop.
